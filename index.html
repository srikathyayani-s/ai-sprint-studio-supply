About AI Sprint Studio

Practical AI. Delivered in Sprints.

AI Sprint Studio is a platform that connects skilled practitioners with companies that need focused AI solutions — fast. We design tightly scoped "AI Sprints": short, outcome-driven engagements that solve real business problems within 1–2 weeks.

On the supply side, that means you. We're looking for engineers, data scientists, ML practitioners, and technical generalists who want to apply their skills to live client projects — not hypothetical exercises. Every sprint you complete is a concrete, demonstrable proof of your capabilities. Projects span knowledge assistants, document pipelines, semantic search, AI copilots, rapid prototypes, and more.

You choose the projects you're excited about. We match you with a senior team, define clear deliverables, and get out of your way. The result is work you're proud to show — and a direct path to continued collaboration with the companies you help.
